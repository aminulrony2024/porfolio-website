Privacy Policy – Quit Fap - No PMO

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Quit Fap - No PMO ("the App") is designed to help users track their personal progress and build better habits. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifying information.

Information related to your progress, preferences, and app settings may be stored locally on your device to provide the App's functionality.

2. Device Permissions

The App may request permission to send notifications and may use your device's biometric authentication feature when you choose to use those features.

Biometric authentication is handled by the Android operating system. The App does not receive or store your fingerprint or other biometric data.

3. Data Sharing

The current version of the App does not send your locally stored progress data to our servers or sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

Your progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can delete this information by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published at the same privacy-policy URL.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through the contact page.
