Privacy Policy

No PMO - Quit Fap Pro (Android app) · Effective date: October 12, 2026

In short:No PMO - Quit Fap Pro keeps everything on your device. We do not have accounts, we do not collect your data, and we cannot see your streaks, check-ins, notes, or photos.

This policy explains what the No PMO - Quit Fap Pro app (“the app”) stores, how it is used, and the choices you have. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developers of the app.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • Streak start and end times, and the reasons you type when you restart.
  • Daily check-ins: mood, urge level, triggers, and any note you write.
  • A count of urges you got through using SOS mode.
  • Rank names, day targets, and any photos you choose for ranks.
  • App settings, such as app lock, reminder time, and your custom triggers.

This information is stored in the app’s private storage. Other apps cannot read it, and we do not receive it.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not upload your streaks, check-ins, notes, or photos to any server.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, because we do not have it.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: used only for the optional daily reminder you turn on. The wording is neutral, and the notification is hidden on the lock screen.
  • App lock (fingerprint, face, or PIN): handled by Android. The app only receives a yes or no result. It never receives or stores your biometric data.
  • Photos: if you choose a photo for a rank, you pick it with the Android photo picker. The app receives only the image you select, makes a resized copy in its private storage, and keeps it on your device.

4. Home screen widget

The optional widget shows your day count, rank, and timer on your home screen. It is drawn from the data stored on your device. When app lock is turned on, the widget shows only “Locked” so your streak is not visible to others.

5. Screenshot blocking

The app blocks screenshots and screen recordings of its own screens to protect your privacy. This also hides the app’s content in the recent apps view.

6. Google Play services

The app uses Google Play features for in-app updates and the in-app review prompt. These are provided and run by Google Play, and Google’s own terms and Privacy Policy apply to them. We do not receive any personal data from these features.

7. Backups, retention, and deletion

  • Your data stays on your device until you remove it.
  • To delete everything, open Settings in the app and choose Erase all data. This clears your streaks, history, check-ins, ranks, and rank photos.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its stored data from your device.
  • Your phone’s own backup service (for example, Android backup to your Google account) may include app data, depending on your device settings. You can manage this in your device settings.

8. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends in part on your device. We recommend using a screen lock and turning on the app lock inside the app.

9. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Not medical advice

The app is a self-help tool. It does not provide medical, psychological, or professional advice and is not a substitute for it. If you are struggling, please consider speaking with a qualified professional or someone you trust.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If the app’s data practices change in a way that affects you, we will update this page before the change takes effect.
